BEFORE YOU BEGIN

How NEXTBIT IRA Works

Review the contribution schedule, interest rules and account conditions before selecting a plan.

01

Choose your plan

IRA Core has a minimum monthly contribution of $200 and a stated rate of 15% monthly. IRA Growth has a minimum monthly contribution of $500 and a stated rate of 25% monthly.

02

Select amount and duration

Choose a monthly amount at or above the plan minimum and a duration of at least one year. The calculator displays estimated total contributions, projected interest and projected balance at maturity.

03

Monthly contributions

Monthly contributions are scheduled for collection from your available USDT balance between the 1st and 10th of each month. Keep sufficient available USDT in your account during this collection window.

04

Monthly interest

When that month's contribution is successfully collected within the 1st–10th window, the account qualifies for that month's interest. Interest is scheduled to be credited on the last day of the month.

05

Missed contributions

If a contribution is not collected by the 10th, the account becomes Pending and does not qualify for that month's interest. The missed month is not automatically added to the maturity date.

06

Account locking and reopening

If the account remains Pending for two consecutive months, it becomes Locked. Reopening requires payment of the outstanding missed contribution amount plus a $50 reopening fee. Reopening is subject to account review.

07

Maturity and withdrawal

The account reaches maturity at the end of the selected duration. The maturity date does not automatically extend because of missed contributions or account locking. Eligible funds become available for withdrawal under the applicable account terms.

Projection information

Projected balances assume every monthly contribution is successfully made at the beginning of its month and monthly interest is compounded on the accumulated eligible balance. Missed contributions reduce the actual outcome. Projections are estimates, not guaranteed results.
